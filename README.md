# AI Governance & Compliance Portfolio

**Independent Project | AI Governance · GRC · ISO/IEC 42001 · SOC 2**

## Project Overview

An independent project demonstrating the practical application of AI governance and compliance principles through six structured artifacts. The portfolio connects governance planning, accountability, gap assessment, control mapping, and evidence management to support responsible AI oversight and compliance readiness.

## Project Artifacts

### 1. AI Governance Program Charter
[01 - Project / Program Charter](01-Project_Program_Charter.pdf)-
Defines program objectives, scope, stakeholders, governance approach, and implementation priorities.

### 2. AI Governance RACI Matrix
[02 - AI Governance RACI Matrix](02-AI_Governance_RACI_Matrix.pdf)-
Maps governance activities to responsible, accountable, consulted, and informed stakeholders to clarify ownership and decision-making.

### 3. Governance Gap Analysis
[03 - Governance Gap Analysis](03-Governance_Gap_Analysis.pdf)-
Assesses governance practices against defined criteria, identifies potential gaps, and prioritizes remediation actions.

### 4. ISO/IEC 42001 Control Matrix
[04 - ISO/IEC 42001 Control Matrix](04-ISO_IEC_42001_Control_Matrix.pdf)-
Maps relevant requirements and applicable controls to implementation activities, ownership, evidence expectations, and status.

### 5. SOC 2 Evidence Tracker
[05 - SOC 2 Evidence Tracker](05-SOC_2_Evidence_Tracker.pdf)-
Organizes control evidence, owners, collection status, review activities, and outstanding actions to support audit readiness.

### 6. Evidence & Lessons Learned
[06 - Evidence & Lessons Learned](06-Evidence_and_Lessons_Learned.pdf)-
Documents supporting evidence, project observations, implementation challenges, and opportunities for continuous improvement.

## Skills Demonstrated

- AI governance and risk management

- Compliance gap analysis and remediation planning
- Governance operating models and RACI development
- Compliance evidence management and audit readiness
- Documentation and continuous improvement

## Frameworks & Methodologies

- ISO/IEC 42001 — AI management systems

## Scope & Disclaimer

This is an independent portfolio project intended to demonstrate practical governance and compliance skills. It is not a formal audit, certification, or attestation of compliance with ISO/IEC 42001 or SOC 2. Assessment scenarios and findings should be interpreted within the documented project scope and assumptions.
